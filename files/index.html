Takatak Zoo

Walk through a 3D jungle. Tap any animal to meet its whole family — father, mother and babies — and spin them all the way round. Then jump into the pond to meet the fish!

About Takatak Zoo

A free 3D zoo for children. No ads, no accounts, nothing about your child leaves this device.
